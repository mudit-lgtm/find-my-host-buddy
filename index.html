Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find out who hosts a website?

Enter the website's domain into our hosting checker. We resolve the DNS, identify the IP address, and match it against our database of known hosting providers.

Is this hosting checker free?

Yes — 100% free, no signup required, with unlimited lookups.

What information does the hosting checker show?

Hosting provider, server IP, physical server location, nameservers, full DNS records (A, AAAA, MX, NS, TXT, CNAME), WHOIS data, domain age, SSL/security headers, and a performance grade.

Why does Cloudflare show as the host?

If a website uses Cloudflare's CDN, DNS resolves to Cloudflare's edge servers. The actual origin host is hidden behind the Cloudflare network.

Can I check the hosting of any website worldwide?

Yes — our tool works with any publicly accessible domain.
